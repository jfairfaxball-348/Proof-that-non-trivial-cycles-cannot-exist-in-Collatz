# RL117 target — legal S-unit gap

For the selected RL109 support, isolate an explicit restriction on legal
coordinates `(a_j,b_j,epsilon_j)` that is absent from arbitrary mixed-base
S-unit sums and is strong enough to yield a proved gap or terminal reduction.

First prove the restriction from ordinary full ownership and zero-flow-cut
order. Then prove an effective two- or three-monomial consequence using that
restriction, not merely `|A log 2-L log 3|` or a generic continued-fraction
estimate. It must reject RL20 at ownership, retain the unscaled ordinary
divisibility against RL79, identify physical rotations for RL81, state where
primitivity enters, and label all finite scans and Raw/Farey subcases.

Stop if the only proposed restriction is true for arbitrary legal supports,
reduces to a slope approximation, or fails to give a nonheuristic gap. Then
begin modular-expander lifting in the binding route order.

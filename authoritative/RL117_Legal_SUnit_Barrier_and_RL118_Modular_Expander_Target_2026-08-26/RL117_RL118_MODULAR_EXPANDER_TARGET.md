# RL118 target — selected-support modular-expander lifting

For the ordered selected support from RL109, seek a deterministic modular
expansion statement at primes with simultaneous dyadic and triadic order
information. The statement must use full ordinary ownership before collapsing
to `D|S`, and it must force a residue conflict or a quantitative
selected-support restriction unavailable to arbitrary subsets of
`{2^a3^b}`.

First specify the prime family, residue graph, transitions induced by legal
ordered swaps, and the exact ownership entry condition. Prove a deterministic
expansion inequality or obstruction with uniform control of all moduli used.
RL20 must fail at `D∤Q`, RL79 must retain the unscaled unit increment, RL81
must keep physical rotations distinct from quotient residues, and primitivity,
Raw/Farey scope, and every finite scan must be explicitly handled.

Stop if the construction is only a density/random-expansion heuristic, is true
for arbitrary subsets, loses ownership at `D|S`, or needs an uncontrolled
prime/modulus family. Then begin p-adic transfer in the binding order.

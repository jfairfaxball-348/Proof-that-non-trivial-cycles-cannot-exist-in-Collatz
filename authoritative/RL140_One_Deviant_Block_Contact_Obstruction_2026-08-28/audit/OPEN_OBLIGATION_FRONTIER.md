# RL140 open obligation frontier

The bounded-interface extension is restricted to one deviant height-one
contact block.  The signed polynomial for two or more deviant blocks is open,
as are arbitrary contact patterns, mixed-height/nonnegative profiles,
negative-defect profiles, multiplicity exclusion, all frontier advances, and
the global gates.

RL122/RL123 run-fibre packing remains unavailable without an actual ordinary
run/state theorem; a defect contact pattern alone does not supply it.

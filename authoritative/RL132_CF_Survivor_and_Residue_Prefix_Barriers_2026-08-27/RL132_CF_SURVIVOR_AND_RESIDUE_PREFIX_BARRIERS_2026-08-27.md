# RL132 — CF-survivor and residue-prefix barriers

## Result classification

RL132 makes no new frontier claim. It records two exact barriers to the two routes specified by RL132's incoming target.

1. The first above-side convergent beyond the external-floor CF gate is `217,976,794,617/137,528,045,312`. Rigorous rational log intervals show that it satisfies the necessary RL19 product/external-floor inequality, so that inequality alone cannot exclude it.
2. A complete fixed-depth residue-prefix descent cannot cover all odd classes: for every `k`, the class `-1 mod 2^k` has `k` initial odd steps and affine slope `(3/2)^k>1`.

The surviving pair is coprime; hence `gcd(A,L)=1` is possible and canonical gcd-block cuts need not exist. For larger gcd, both balanced and strict block count profiles are combinatorially feasible. Thus count-only gcd restrictions also do not eliminate the pair.

These are method barriers, not cycle constructions. The conditional frontier `L>=49,547,666,544`, Gate A/B status, and all correction/demotion entries are unchanged.

# RL119 — conditioned p-adic fibre barrier and route transition

## Outcome and classification

No finite conditioned owned-fibre operator, stationary law, or dangerous-cone
escape theorem is proved. RL119 records an analytic **p-adic ownership
barrier**. For every ordinary denominator

`D=2^A-3^L`, `gcd(D,6)=1`.

Thus at the natural dyadic and triadic levels `D` is a unit. Full ownership
`Q=Dx` does not imply `Q=0 mod 2^k` or `Q=0 mod 3^k`; it merely relates the
p-adic residue of `Q` to the unknown physical state `x`. Consequently a
finite 2-adic or 3-adic parity operator is not conditioned by `D|Q` and is
an unowned model unless a new physical-state law is supplied.

At a prime `p|D`, an owned input does have `Q=0 mod p^k` only up to the
variable valuation `k=v_p(D)`. Retaining complete `D|Q` therefore requires
all variable prime-power factors of `D`, exactly the uncontrolled factor
family identified in RL118. Conditioning on one selected p-adic factor is
strictly weaker than ownership, while conditioning on all factors has no
uniform finite state. No non-random stationary law can be defined from the
stated data without choosing one of those invalid alternatives.

This meets the RL119 stop condition. It does not rule out a future p-adic
argument supplied with a new full-ownership physical law or controlled factor
theorem. No Gate closure, cycle exclusion, or Collatz result is claimed.

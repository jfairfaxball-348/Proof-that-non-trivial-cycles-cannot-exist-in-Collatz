# RL204 — strategic review, audit, roadmap and progress assessment

Date: 2026-08-31

## Executive decision

**Decision: pivot now to a focused global closure route tournament in RL205.**

This is not a reset and not a demotion of the H21 programme. The current H21 route has
made real structural progress, but RL203 has reached a precise information boundary for
the mechanism currently being consumed. Spending the next session on another automatic
increment of the same consumer has lower expected closure leverage than comparing the
strongest still-live global routes under common red teams.

RL205 must preserve H21 as a first-class contender. H21 should win the tournament only
if it can name a concrete source of **independent owned information** beyond the RL203
shallow dyadic/root-anchor boundary and an operational success criterion.

## 2. Assessment of the present H21 programme

### What the recent run genuinely bought

The RL193–RL203 lineage tightened the route from broad physical/phase information toward
an exact H21 local interface: phase incidence, rank/height occupation, zero-edge
ownership/placement, H21 selection, prehistory, oriented lift/terminal defect, K-rank
refinement, exact endpoint moment, root-anchor collision, and finally the dyadic
information boundary. These are reusable constraints, not discarded work.

### Rate of direct closure progress

The most recent generations have principally refined **where information can come from**
rather than deleting the remaining necessary set. In the frozen RL203 state the rank
count is unchanged, all four eta classes remain, and no state/sign/physical-charge/Gate
conclusion is selected. This is an important warning against measuring progress only by
number of exact lemmas.

### Exact bottleneck exposed by RL203

To combine a fresh eta residue with the inherited Hensel exclusion, normalized endpoint
information must reach `2^56`. The shallow dyadic prefix supplies only already-owned
parity information. On the proved `a<p` side, the named root-prefix contribution starts
at depth at least 63, so it cannot independently provide those bits at that scale.

Therefore the next H21 theorem cannot simply push the same shallow truncation harder.
It must own information from a different source: a rigorously analysed `a>p` geometry,
deep post-terminal completion, an independent complete-word relation, or a genuinely
global physical/ownership coupling.

### Realistic chain from H21 to closure

Even a successful new eta/sign selector would not by itself close RL. A defensible chain
still requires, in some order:

1. physical H21 incidence or an exhaustive charging/ownership theorem;
2. independent terminal information strong enough to delete ranks/select a state or
   force a contradiction;
3. closure of the sole surviving branch under the exact scope locks;
4. a valid Gate/global lifting step.

That chain is viable, but it is longer than the current local target alone suggests.

## 3. Strongest viable alternatives

The targeted historical audits identify several live families that are not superseded by
RL203.

### Route A — `D|Q`-sensitive owned Gate-B bridge

Seek an owned close pair, strict excursion, or non-coboundary residual producing a true
full-modulus contradiction or manufacturing every hypothesis of the already-closed
radius-3 theorem. It has very high closure leverage because an exhaustive bridge can
close Gate B directly. It must pass the historical coboundary/proper-factor/size and
`D∤Q`-countermodel tests.

### Route B — H21 independent-information completion

Retain the present route but change the consumer: `a>p`, deep post-terminal complete-word
completion, physical H21 charge, or another independent global coupling. The recent
machinery gives it high reuse value, but the new consumer must demonstrate independence
rather than repackage the endpoint identity/tail.

### Route C — ordinary-`+1` non-homogeneous global bridge

Revisit extremal/physical/basin ideas only in a form that uses the genuine `+1` rule
non-homogeneously and therefore rejects generalized-increment fake realizations. This has
high potential globality and avoids being tied to the sole H21 branch.

### Route D — owned zero-area / active-weight / macro packing

The RL72/RL75 lineage sought a full-phase ownership theorem that prices synchronized
zero-area depth or active-rank weight. Such a theorem could unlock Gate-A defect/packing
machinery. It remains globally attractive, but prior work exposed difficult uniform
context control, so duplication risk is significant.

### Route E — Farey/cascade lifting, not raw scan extension

The physical/2-adic cascade remains valuable branch evidence. More depth alone is not
ranked highly until a first-Farey/branch-domination or finite global cover theorem makes
that compute directly global. Resume raw scanning only on such a trigger or if a new
analysis proves a credible finite exhaustive frontier.

## 5. RL205 tournament rule and success criteria

RL205 should run a **focused tournament**, not a fresh 200-session audit. Use RL72, RL75,
RL99 and RL203 as the strategic baselines and recover only exact live interfaces needed
for a candidate probe.

Apply the same red teams to every route:

- Does it own the global/full-phase hypothesis it consumes?
- Does it distinguish genuine ordinary `+1` dynamics from generalized-increment fakes?
- For Gate B, is it genuinely `D|Q` sensitive and non-coboundary?
- Is the proposed information independent rather than a rewritten endpoint/tail law?
- Does success remove a global obligation, not merely deepen a branch sieve?

H21 may be selected for RL206 only if the tournament identifies a concrete independent
consumer and a one-session measurable success condition such as: a nonzero rank deletion,
eta/state/sign selection from independent data, a physical H21 incidence/charge theorem,
or a branch contradiction. Failure to identify such a consumer keeps H21 preserved but
deferred.

The Gate-B route may be selected if it produces a candidate residual/owned pair that
passes the historical countermodel/coboundary tests and has a plausible exhaustive path.

## 6. Answer to the RL204 central question

The current programme is **not wasted work and is realistically connected to closure**, but
the exact mechanism just used is no longer the best automatic next use of a session.
RL203 proves that the shallow dyadic/root-anchor consumer has exhausted the information it
can independently provide on the proved below-p side. The correct response is not to
abandon H21; it is to compare it now against global alternatives and demand a new
independent-information edge before resuming it.

Therefore: **pivot now to RL205 route tournament.**

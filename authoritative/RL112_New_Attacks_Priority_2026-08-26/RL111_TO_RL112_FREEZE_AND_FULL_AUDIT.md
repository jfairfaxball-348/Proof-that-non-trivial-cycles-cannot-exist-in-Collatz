The audit traversed every tracked session root (`RL00`, `RL02`, `RL05`,
`RL07`, `RL10`–`RL111` where present) and Archive package.  Duplicated
inherited copies are represented once by their canonical earliest cited source.
The registry intentionally records only claims which a later proof-state
ledger labels analytic, exact finite, or externally inherited.  A heading
containing “lemma”, “theorem”, or “target” is *not* evidence of establishment:
targets, conditional statements, simulations, and barriers keep their stated
classification.

This is a verification-economy audit.  The current verified authoritative
bundle was checked; historical expensive certificates were not recursively
rerun, because no current dependency failure or contradiction was found.

## 4. Strategy attack surface — why earlier directions stalled

| Strategy family | What it consumes | Frozen bottleneck / reason it stalls |
|---|---|---|
| Parity-word, rotation, and quotient algebra | `D=2^A-3^L`, word numerators, rotation congruences | Most normalized identities are all-word tautologies or collapse to `S=D(x_v-x_u)`; ordinary full-`D` ownership is the missing non-scalable input. |
| Local radius / finite-state grammar | bounded closest-pair support or terminal grammar | Radius three is locally closed, but no valid global bridge forces radius at most three; larger case ladders were retired. |
| Farey / continued-fraction cascades | near-resonant slope and finite interval propagation | certified only under exact first-Farey, raw `g=1`, full-phase assumptions; raw multiplicity and global scope cannot be added silently. |
| Multiplicity cylinders and excursions | first-surplus blocks, physical representatives | normalized cylinder compression has no absolute-scale consumer; equal-slope excursion is conditional. |
| Numerator content and prime/local lifting | ordinary `+1` prefix/suffix factors | prefix/suffix, inverse-cylinder, prime-prefix and roof-factor splices are covariant or leave a scalable content factor. |
| Physical-basin equality / roofs | odd cores, roof depth, physical representatives | equality classification is rigid locally, but it does not yield a global ordinary-basin equality bridge. |
| Sparse owned rotation transport | actual rotations with full `D|Q` | it produces a nonzero `D`-multiple, but unrestricted geometry leaves coefficient scale above `D`; a support-selection or physical-strip theorem remains missing. |
| Density, random-drift, stopping-time work | statistical orbit behavior | “almost all” does not control the exceptional cycle required here.  Tao’s logarithmic-density result is deliberately not a cycle exclusion. |
| Generalized Collatz simulations | broad affine-map families | Conway-type universality is a boundary condition: a proposed **uniform algorithm for arbitrary generalized maps** is not a viable route.  It does not forbid a theorem using special arithmetic of the fixed `3x+1` map. |

The common mathematical obstruction is mixed-base alignment: the cycle
equations couple powers of `2` and `3`, while legal parity constraints decide
which combinations occur.  A candidate is admissible only if it attacks this
coupling, rather than replacing it with a density heuristic or a normalized
identity.

## 5. External context checked for this audit

Tao proves that almost every orbit (in logarithmic density) drops below any
given divergent bound; it is a strong density theorem, not a universal orbit
or cycle theorem.  Terras’s stopping-time work is likewise an essential
statistical baseline.  Lagarias’s bibliography/survey was used to ensure the
strategy map is not presented as a replacement for the literature.  Conway’s
result concerns sufficiently generalized iteration systems, so the correct
filter is universality of the proposed method, not an assertion that every
Collatz-specific idea would decide an undecidable family.

Sources: Tao, *Almost all orbits of the Collatz map attain almost bounded
values*, arXiv:1909.03562; Terras, *A stopping time problem on the positive
integers*, Acta Arith. 30 (1976); Lagarias, *The 3x+1 Problem: An Overview*,
arXiv:2111.02635; Conway, *Unpredictable iterations* (1972), as catalogued by
Lagarias.

## 6. Mandatory filters for RL112 and RL113

Every proposed attack must pass, before deep work:

1. **Universality filter:** state precisely why it uses a feature special to
   ordinary `3x+1`, and whether it would incorrectly imply a decision method
   for generalized Collatz maps.
2. **Mixed-base contact test:** name the exact assertion about `2`/`3`
   alignment, a support set, or a physical representative that the method
   would establish.
3. **Cheap falsification:** test the assertion on the first several thousand
   primitive parity words and the RL20 `D∤Q` fake where applicable.  A passed
   finite test is evidence only.  Also apply the RL81 physical-versus-quotient
   separation: no state is physical without actual full `D|Q` ownership.
4. **Lineage test:** identify whether the candidate is merely a relabelling of
   a frozen route in section 4.  Preserve the Raw/Farey branch qualification;
   no branch-only statement is a global conclusion.
5. **Closable-subproblem test:** specify a finite or fully quantified theorem
   whose proof would be useful even if the global conjecture remains open.

The audit target is to run these filters and form a separate RL113 tournament;
RL112 itself does not select a winner or advance a candidate as a theorem.

# RL118 — prime-factor ownership barrier and route transition

## Outcome and classification

RL118 establishes no modular-expansion inequality. It records an analytic
**barrier for the direct prime-residue formulation**. Ordinary ownership is
`D|Q`, with variable `D=2^A-3^L`. A modulus `m` yields the zero-residue
condition `Q=0 mod m` from ownership only when `m|D`. If `p` does not divide
`D`, then `Q=Dx` imposes no fixed zero condition modulo `p`, because `D` is a
unit modulo `p` and the physical integer `x` is otherwise unconstrained by
this congruence.

For a selected family of prime powers with product `M|D`, their joint test is
only `M|Q`. It is strictly weaker than `D|Q` whenever `M<D`: choosing
`Q=M` gives the same zero residues but not `D|Q`. Therefore an exact modular
ownership test requires prime-power moduli whose product contains all of `D`.
There is no inherited global bound or controlled factor family for the live
variable denominator. A graph built from primes dividing `D` consequently
needs an uncontrolled factor/modulus family; a graph at external primes does
not enter through ownership; and a graph using only `D|S` has lost ownership.

This meets the RL118 stop condition. It does not prove that every possible
modular-expander theorem is false: a later route would need a new theorem
controlling a sufficient factor of `D` on the actual selected-support fibre.
No such input is established here. The next binding route is p-adic transfer.

## Red teams

- **RL20:** its `D∤Q` status is detectable only after enough prime-power
  factors of its particular `D` are retained; a geometry-only residue graph
  does not provide the required ownership rejection.
- **RL79:** generalized ownership is `D|sQ`, not `D|Q`; no factor `s` is
  cancelled in the prime-power conditions.
- **RL81:** residues are not physical states. `Q/D` is physical only under
  complete ordinary ownership.
- **Primitivity:** it remains the inherited source of distinct rotations and
  `S!=0`; it supplies no factor control for `D`.
- **Scope and finite work:** no Raw/Farey restriction or finite scan is used.

## Dependencies and next route

This barrier uses RL109 full ordinary ownership and the RL115/RL116 variable
modulus state observation. It changes no inherited theorem. Begin RL119 with
the conditioned p-adic-transfer target; do not revive a prime graph without a
proved controlled ownership-bearing factor family.

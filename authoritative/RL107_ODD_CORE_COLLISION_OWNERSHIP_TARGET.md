# RL107 target — odd-core collisions at internal primitive boundaries

Use the RL106 aligned all-even-run exclusion as an ordinary physical input.
For the actual boundary states `x_p,x_(2p),...,x_((g-1)p)`, test whether equal
odd cores force a pure-even cyclic segment whose aligned-block support is a
forbidden long run, or whether short-run collisions can be classified exactly.

Success requires a genuine ordinary full-ownership consequence: an injective
map to distinct odd product phases with a quantitative budget, an exact basin
equality, or another un-divided denominator-sensitive relation.  A mere
generalized-increment population inequality, residue condition, rotation
transport, inverse-cylinder covariance, CRT match, or finite basin-time
observation is not success.

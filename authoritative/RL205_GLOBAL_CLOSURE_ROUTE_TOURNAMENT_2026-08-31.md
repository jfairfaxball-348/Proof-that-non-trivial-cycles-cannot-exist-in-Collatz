## 2. Exact ownership probe used by the tournament

This is an **inherited corollary/interface observation**, not a new theorem promotion.

Let the usual full-word quantities be

`D = 2^A - 3^L`

and let `Q_m` be the numerator of the `m`-th cyclic rotation. The inherited rotation
transport has the form

`2^m Q_m - 3^P_m Q_0 = D Q(prefix_m)`.

Under genuine full-`D` ownership `D | Q_0`, rotation gcd invariance gives `D | Q_m` for
every phase. Hence the quotient-layer variables

`R_m := Q_m / D`

are integers and satisfy

`2^m R_m - 3^P_m R_0 = Q(prefix_m)`.

At one step this is exactly the ordinary affine update

`2 R_(m+1) = R_m` when the current bit is `0`, and

`2 R_(m+1) = 3 R_m + 1` when the current bit is `1`.

Therefore **division by `D` exposes the owned physical integer layer**. The historical
`D∤Q` radius-4 fake cannot supply this integer quotient layer. RL79's modulo-`D` rank-one
collapse explicitly does not rule out constructions after division by `D` or other
quotient-sensitive data.

This probe does not itself create a contradiction. It does identify a concrete place where
Gate-B work can consume ownership information that the known fake lacks.

### A. Gate-B quotient-layer owned residual — selected

smallest residual family `W` whose definition or divisibility step uses the integer

rotation classes `Q_m mod D`.

**Independence.** Quotient integrality is unavailable to the `D∤Q` local fake and lies
outside the same-modulus polynomial collapse identified by RL79.

**Ownership/globality.** The input `D|Q` is a full-word ownership condition. A successful
exhaustive witness bridge can remove Gate B, rather than merely refine one H21 branch.

**Required red teams.**
- RL20 `D∤Q` fake must fail an essential premise, not merely a later inequality.
- `W` must not reduce to a cyclic coboundary or a proper-factor endpoint potential.

  contradiction or an exhaustive bounded configuration reduction.

  are recovered from the live interface.

**Smallest proof step.** Establish an owned quotient-excursion interface lemma for the
canonical Gate-B witness and prove that one explicit residual family is simultaneously
full-ownership-sensitive and non-coboundary.

**One-session success for RL206.** Either:
1. prove the exact divisibility plus strict bound `0 < |W| < M` for a canonical witness

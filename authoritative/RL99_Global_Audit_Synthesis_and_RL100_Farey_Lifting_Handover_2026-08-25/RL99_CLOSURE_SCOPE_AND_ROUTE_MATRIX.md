## 5. Cascade closure economics

Frozen branch:

- live lower endpoint: `2,921,801,523`;
- upper endpoint: `42,150,931,559`;
- numerical width: `39,229,130,036`;
- odd candidates in the inclusive branch window: about `19,614,565,019`.

Recent RL97 marginal observation:

- deep radius: `2.4M -> 3.0M`;
- first-live-`k` gain: `26,792`;
- crude recent marginal rate: about `0.04465` units of `k` per deep-radius unit.

A linear extrapolation of that one recent regime gives roughly `8.8e11` additional deep-radius units to cover the remaining numerical width. This is **diagnostic only**, not a theorem: geometry, new tiers, floor changes, or a stronger charging theorem can change the rate.

The current route therefore has excellent **local certifiability and monotone accumulation**, but poor **global economics without a structural lift or acceleration**.

## 3. Positive-carry three-clock theorem

`epsilon=kappa H+r`, `0<r<H`,

`d=gcd(H,epsilon)`, `H=dh`, `epsilon=dE`, `r=d rho`.

`e=v2(E)`, `t=v2(rho)=v2(r)`.

- `e<t` implies `gamma=e`;
- `t<e` implies `gamma=t`;
- `e=t` implies `gamma>e`.

RL319 proves `0<kappa<2^34`, hence `gamma<=33`. Therefore

`min(e,t)<=33`,                                         (3.2)

`e=t<=32`.                                              (3.3)

support-independent cap on the first interface among the three paths.

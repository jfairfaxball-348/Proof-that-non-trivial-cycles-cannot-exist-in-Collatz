# RL215 research report — complementary-arc sandwich

Date: 2026-09-01.

## Target attacked

RL215 was instructed to consume RL214's 1,629,818,931 bounded e=16 arithmetic root
candidates using a genuinely independent complete-word datum, preferably `Q modD^2`
or an independently anchored full-arc numerator condition.

## Main advance

The successful consumer is the **complementary return arc** rather than a new
D-adic digit.

RL214 used positivity of the first p-arc numerator to upper-bound `y_0`.
RL215 uses positivity of the distinct remaining `z=L-p` arc, together with the
complete-cycle return `y_L=y_0` and root gap `y_p-y_0=K0`, to prove the opposite
inequality

`(2^(A-u)/3^z-1)y_0>K0`.

An exact rational logarithmic bound converts this to

`y_0>=24,913,843,845,551,577,787,381`.

This creates a certified two-sided root window.

## Exact finite consequence

Projecting the lower bound onto the RL214 arithmetic progressions leaves
331,935,455 candidates before the terminal valuation and **331,935,285** after it.
This removes **1,297,883,646** post-terminal arithmetic candidates relative to RL214,
about 79.63%, with no 1.63-billion brute-force scan.

No H21 prefix is deleted. Both states, all four mod18 classes and all 469 reachable
mod2187 classes remain. The global necessary-rank frontier therefore remains
13,415,865,871.

## Additional route checked

A global nonnegative-height roof bound on the root p-arc numerator was derived first
and already gave a substantial lower root bound. A shifted version at e=16 was then
checked. The complementary-return positivity bound is stronger and dominates those
roof projections at the current k scale, so they are not promoted as separate
consumers.

## Strategic consequence

The finite e=16 family is now only about one fifth of its RL214 size, but interval
information alone still leaves thousands of lifts per prefix. The next useful datum
must be discrete or couple a second absolute phase: `Q modD^2`, a complementary
numerator residue/valuation, or a newly proved e=16 terminal/successor signed-K
anchor. Merely sharpening the logarithmic constants cannot delete a prefix at the
present progression spacing.

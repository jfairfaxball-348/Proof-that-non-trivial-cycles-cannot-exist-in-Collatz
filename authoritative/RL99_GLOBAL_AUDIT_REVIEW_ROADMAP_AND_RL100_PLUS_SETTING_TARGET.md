- Does the theorem consume `s=1` non-homogeneously?

- If it survives the fake family unchanged, can it really close RL?

### Recent blue/dyadic/cascade programme

Determine whether the current four-tier cascade is:

- merely an increasingly strong finite/branch sieve;

### Closure economics

For each serious route estimate:

- how much of the remaining domain it can eliminate;
- whether its progress is monotone/cumulative;
- what certifiable compute it requires;
- what structural bottleneck appears before global closure;
- whether a new theorem can remove that bottleneck.

# RL150 — Multi-excursion local-independence barrier

## Status

**Proved analytic method barrier** for the inherited local defect recurrence.
No cycle branch is excluded.

For any two nonadjacent mechanical positions `j,k` whose outgoing increments
are `c_j=c_k=2`, define a defect profile with `h_j=h_k=-1` and `h_i=0`
otherwise.  With `a_i=c_i+h_i-h_(i+1)`, every exponent is positive: entries
have exponent `c_(j-1)+1` or `c_(k-1)+1`, each negative phase exits with
exponent one, and all remaining phases use the mechanical increment.

Thus the recurrence permits two separated isolated negative-excursion
templates without a relation between their positions, entries, exits, or
cyclic order.  Each actual excursion separately supplies the RL136/RL148
physical fibres, but the inherited results supply no equation joining two such
triples.  Their numerical maps are individually injective, not a cross-
excursion return or CRT invariant.

This template is not asserted to have ordinary full ownership or to be a
cycle.  It proves only that a new nonlocal ownership/ordering theorem is
needed; the local recurrence and existing one-excursion fibres cannot create
one by concatenation.

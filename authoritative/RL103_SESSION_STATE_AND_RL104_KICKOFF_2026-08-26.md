# RL103 session state and RL104 kickoff

RL103 freezes the direct affine use of the first-surplus maximum cylinder.
For every generalized increment `s`, an inverse prefix yields the exact
physical congruence `M == s c_h (mod 2*3^o)`.  In the owned case `s=1`, this
is the ordinary cylinder but does not carry an additional factor of the full
denominator.  No lower-content theorem or global claim is obtained.

RL104 must therefore avoid affine inverse congruences, rotation transport,
and homogeneous state scaling.  Its task is a nonlinear or prime-local
absolute `s=1` content mechanism, if one exists.

# RL141 completion review

RL141 proves a full-ownership obstruction for a height-one contact pattern
that agrees with a repeated block outside a consecutive interface of `ell<X`
blocks, provided `g>=ell+3`.  The proof combines the exact RL140 contact
factor with a 2-adic valuation-band non-cancellation and a strict size bound.

The outcome does not cover wrapping or scattered support, interfaces of length
at least `X`, profiles with too few repeated blocks outside the interface,
higher/negative defects, or multiplicities.  No global claim is made.

# RL150 target — negative-excursion cyclic order

Work only with realised isolated excursions.  Test whether cyclic order of
their negative states, entries, and exits imposes a return, spacing, or
injection constraint not visible at one excursion.  Preserve actual-state
ownership and do not replace it with candidate shell counts.  If no new
nonlocal invariant emerges, freeze that obstruction and pivot away from the
negative-depth route.

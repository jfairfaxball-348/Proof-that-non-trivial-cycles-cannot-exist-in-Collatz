# RL115 target — dyadic–triadic Fourier discrepancy on owned supports

## Objective

Work only on the next retained RL113 route. For the canonical zero-flow-cut
closest owned rotations from RL109, with `R_*=R>=4`, let

`S=Q(v)-Q(u)=sum_(j=1)^R epsilon_j 2^(a_j)3^(b_j)`,

where `D=2^A-3^L`, `D|Q(u)`, `D|Q(v)`, and `0!=S` is a full unscaled
`D`-multiple. Define an ownership-sensitive dyadic–triadic character
statistic for the *selected ordered support* and seek a strict coefficient-mass
loss relative to RL110's all-legal maximum.

## First mandatory lemma

Specify a finite character group or a precisely controlled family of residue
characters, its statistic on the zero-flow-cut support, and its dependence on
the full owned conditions `D|Q(u)=D|Q(v)=0`. Prove that the statistic changes
when those conditions are replaced by the RL20 fake's nonzero numerator
residue, and that it supplies a quantitative selected-support mass loss not
valid for arbitrary fixed-content words.

The following do **not** satisfy the obligation: a character identity true
for every word of fixed `(A,L)`; a statement recovered after replacing the
owned condition by `D|S`; a random-support heuristic; or a character modulus
whose size/state is silently allowed to grow without a proved uniform control.

## Mandatory red teams

1. RL20 must fail at the actual ownership entry condition, not merely at
   radius or an unconditioned character statistic.
2. Retain ordinary `D|Q` and `D|S` unscaled; never cancel a generalized
   increment factor (RL79).
3. Identify physical rotations before interpreting `Q/D` as a state (RL81).
4. State where primitivity makes the selected pair distinct and `S!=0`.
5. Label Raw/Farey material as branch-specific if it is introduced.
6. Label every scan with its exact complete range; scans do not prove a
   Fourier inequality or an inverse theorem.

## Stop and handoff

Record a barrier and stop this route if the strongest statistic is invariant
under increment scaling, holds for arbitrary fixed-content supports, has only
heuristic expansion, or needs an unbounded modulus/state to retain `D|Q`.
On that outcome begin the next surviving route, the two-base finite
transducer, preserving the RL113 fallback order.

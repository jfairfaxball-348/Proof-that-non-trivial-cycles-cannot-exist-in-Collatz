# RL140 completion review

RL140 extends the repeated-mechanical numerator method to a nontrivial
bounded-interface family.  The proof retains the exact ordinary affine
numerator and full denominator factor, and the finite algebra checker passes.

The conclusion is deliberately narrow: a height-one contact pattern may not
be block-periodic except for one deviant block for `g>=3`; at `g=2` the same
holds only when the deviant block changes at most three contact residues.
Arbitrary nonperiodic patterns, higher defects, negative defects, multiplicity
exclusion, frontiers, Gate A/B, RL, and Collatz remain open.

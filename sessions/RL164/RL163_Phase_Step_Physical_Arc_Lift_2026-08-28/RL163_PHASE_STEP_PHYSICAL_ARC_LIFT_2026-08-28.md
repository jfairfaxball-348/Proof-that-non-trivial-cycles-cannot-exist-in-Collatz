## 1. Scope

## 2. RL163.1 — physical exponent of a phase edge

Define

`p=A^(-1) mod L=65,470,613,321`,

`u=(Ap-1)/L=103,768,467,013`.

Thus `Ap=uL+1`. Let `sigma(j)=j+p mod L`, and let

`B_j=S_(j+p)-S_j=sum_(t=0)^(p-1)a_(j+t mod L)`

be the exponent total of the actual forward `p`-step arc from `y_j` to `y_(sigma(j))`.

### Theorem RL163.1

For every `j mod L`,

`B_j=u+1_(j=L-p)+h_j-h_(sigma(j))`.                    (2.1)

Here `L-p=72,057,431,991`.

### Proof

For `r_j=Aj mod L`, lifted floors and `Ap=uL+1` give

`b_(j+p)-b_j=u+1_(r_j=L-1)`.

The residues are a permutation. Since `A(L-p)=-Ap=-1 mod L`, the exceptional residue occurs uniquely at `j=L-p`. Substitute `S=b-h` to obtain (2.1). The lifted definitions make this valid when the arc crosses the chosen physical cut.

This is the corrected bridge demanded by RL162: a phase neighbour is a long physical arc, not a chronological neighbour.

## 3. RL163.2 — ordinary affine content

Iterating `3y+1` on the arc gives

`2^(B_j)y_(sigma(j))=3^p y_j+C_j`,

where

`C_j=sum_(t=0)^(p-1) 3^(p-1-t) 2^(a_j+...+a_(j+t-1))`.

The empty exponent is zero. Hence `C_j>0`; its first term is odd and all later terms are even, so `C_j` is odd. The complementary arc gives

`y_j < (2^(B_j)/3^p)y_(sigma(j)) < lambda y_j`,

where `lambda=2^A/3^L`.

The positive odd `C_j` is genuine physical information absent from an unordered phase polynomial.

## 4. RL163.3 — aggregate limitation

Summing (2.1) over all `j mod L` gives

`sum_j B_j=Lu+1=pA`.

The defect differences telescope, the single carry accounts for `Ap-uL=1`, and independently each `a_i` occurs in exactly `p` cyclic arcs. Therefore the aggregate phase-step law supplies no endpoint-independent capacity bound and no exclusion. This is a barrier only to arguments using its aggregate, not to a content-sensitive use of `C_j`.

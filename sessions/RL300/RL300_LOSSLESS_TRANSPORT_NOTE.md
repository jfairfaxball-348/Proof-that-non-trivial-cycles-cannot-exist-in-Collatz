# RL300 — lossless transport note

The promoted `sessions/RL300/` directory carries the complete handover as individual Git blobs:

- report;
- proof/scope ledger;
- external-delay provenance;
- exact resonance-record certificate;
- closeout;
- successor target;
- incoming-target snapshot;
- fresh verification log;
- portable fast verifier and verifier README;
- internal SHA256 manifest.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` hashes every promoted handover payload except itself.

Closeout reconstructed these files in clean temporary storage, verified the internal manifest, and reran `verification/verify_rl300_fast.py` from that reconstruction.

The external exhaustive delay-record completeness assertion is deliberately not represented as internally replayed computation. Its exact provenance and consumer range are frozen in `RL300_EXTERNAL_DELAY_CERTIFICATE.md`.

Knowledge catalogues are `stale/deferred` under connector policy and are not proof-state authority.

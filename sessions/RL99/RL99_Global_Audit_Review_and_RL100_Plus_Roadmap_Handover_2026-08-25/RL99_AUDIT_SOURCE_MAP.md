## Audit cautions

1. A historical session name is not proof status.
2. A later frozen ledger supersedes an earlier stronger interpretation when a correction exists.
3. “Verified computationally” is not automatically “proved analytically”.
4. A branch-specific interval elimination is not a global Gate-A theorem.
5. A theorem homogeneous under generalized odd increment `s` does not by itself distinguish genuine Collatz ownership `s=1`.
6. A fake/countermodel route should be recorded as a method barrier, not silently forgotten.
7. Exact finite certificates are legitimate mathematical assets when their domain and verifier provenance are explicit.
8. The purpose of the audit is not to punish computational routes; it is to identify what would make one exhaustive.

# RL120 — non-homogeneous cocycle barrier and return-policy transition

## Outcome and classification

RL120 proves no new physical size bound. It records an analytic **cocycle
barrier** for the stated diagnostic. At an odd shortcut step the exact affine
equation is

`2x' - 3x = 1`,

and for generalized odd increment it is `2x'-3x=s`; an even step is
`2x'-x=0`. Any linear combination of step equations has an increment term
equal to `s` times its ordinary term. Thus a cocycle constructed solely from
these affine equations either scales with `s`, failing the RL79 requirement,
or, after eliminating physical states over the rotation word, becomes the
known scaled numerator/rotation transport identity.

Retaining actual states does keep the ordinary constant `1`, but an identity
alone gives no independent inequality: the generalized cycle state and its
ordinary state scale together with `s`, and RL110 supplies no physical strip
or size force on the selected states. No separate force was derived. This
meets the RL120 stop condition.

All retained RL113 routes have now closed as formulation-specific barriers.
The binding next action is the RL113 return policy, not a revival of inverse,
Fourier, transducer, S-unit, modular, p-adic, or cocycle language.

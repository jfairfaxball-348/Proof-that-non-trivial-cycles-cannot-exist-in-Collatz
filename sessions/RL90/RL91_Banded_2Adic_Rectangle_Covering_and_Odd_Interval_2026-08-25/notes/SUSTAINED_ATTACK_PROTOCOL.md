# Sustained attack protocol

Adopted 2026-08-25 from RL89 onward.

A mathematical milestone is a checkpoint, not a session stop trigger.

After every new theorem/certificate/bound:

1. propagate it through every surviving case;
2. sharpen case-specific constants;
3. push to the next admissible parameter value or interval;
4. splice with the inherited proof-state ledger;
5. red-team against known barriers;
6. test the strongest adjacent continuation.

Close only when context/compute pressure threatens reliability, a stop-and-repair event occurs, the route plus its natural adjacent continuations are rigorously exhausted, or the target is actually closed and immediate consequences are propagated.

Verification economy remains mandatory: use the added session budget for new mathematics, not recursive historical re-verification.

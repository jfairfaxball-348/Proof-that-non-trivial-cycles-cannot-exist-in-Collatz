# RL205 target — global closure route tournament and independent-information selection

Date: 2026-08-31

RL205 is a focused route-selection/theorem-probe session. It is not a recursive historical
audit, not a mathematical reset, and not permission to demote the H21 programme.

## Central target

Choose the next mathematical attack by testing the strongest live closure routes against
the same ownership, independence, countermodel and globality criteria. Produce one exact
RL206 target with a credible route to removing a Gate/global obligation.

## Required contenders

1. `D|Q`-sensitive owned Gate-B close-pair / strict-excursion / non-coboundary residual.
2. H21 independent-information completion: rigorously `a>p`, deep post-terminal
   complete-word data, physical H21 incidence/charge, or another independent global
   coupling beyond the RL203 shallow/root-anchor boundary.
3. Ordinary-`+1` non-homogeneous extremal/physical/basin bridge that discriminates the
   generalized-increment fake family.
4. Full-phase ownership control of zero-area depth/active weight or owned-macro packing.
5. Farey/cascade branch-to-global lifting; do not rank raw scan depth as global progress
   without a proved coverage/domination mechanism.

## Tournament method

Use RL203 as the current proof-state authority and use RL72, RL75 and RL99 only as
strategic route-map baselines. Recover older exact interfaces selectively when a concrete
candidate needs them. Do not rerun historical expensive certificates without a live
reason.

For each contender, state:

- exact theorem/certificate target;
- exact inherited inputs and scope;
- why its new information is independent;
- global or branch-specific ownership;
- strongest known countermodel/method barrier;
- smallest plausible proof step;
- measurable one-session success condition;
- failure/pivot condition;
- what Gate/global obligation would disappear if the route succeeds.

A short theorem probe is encouraged where it sharply discriminates routes. Do not spend
RL205 running a long incremental attack before route selection is complete.

## Mandatory red teams

- **Ownership:** no branch/local condition silently becomes global.
- **Independence:** no endpoint/tail/coboundary restatement is counted as a new equation.
- **Gate B:** candidate must genuinely consume full `D|Q`/full-phase information and fail
  appropriately on inherited `D∤Q` local countermodels.
- **Ordinary +1:** candidate must use `s=1` non-homogeneously enough to reject generalized
  increment fake realizations.
- **H21:** no unproved `a<p` to `a>p` reflection; no claim of physical H21 charge without
  an ownership/exhaustivity theorem.
- **Cascade:** no amount of branch scan depth is called closure without coverage.

## Selection rule

Prefer the route with the highest combination of global closure leverage, proof ownership,
independent information, tractability and low duplication risk — not simply the route with
the largest existing code base.

H21 may be selected for RL206 only with a concrete independent consumer and a measurable
success criterion: at least one of a certified rank deletion, independently forced
eta/state/sign, physical H21 incidence/charge, or a branch contradiction.

The Gate-B route may be selected if a candidate owned residual/close-pair survives the
coboundary/proper-factor/quotient-size/countermodel tests and has an exhaustive path.

## Completion

RL205 is complete when it freezes:

1. a ranked route matrix after probes;
2. explicit dead/blocked reasons for routes not selected;
3. one primary RL206 theorem target with success and pivot rules;
4. preservation instructions for every viable deferred route.

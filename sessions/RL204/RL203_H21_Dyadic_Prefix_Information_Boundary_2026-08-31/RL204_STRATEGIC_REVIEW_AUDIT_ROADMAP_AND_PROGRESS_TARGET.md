# RL204 target — strategic review, audit, road map and progress assessment

Date: 2026-08-31.

This is a deliberate review session, not a mathematical reset and not a
demotion of the current H21 programme.

## Central question

Answer explicitly:

> Is what we are working on the best use of our time, is it realistically
> leading to closing RL, or do we need a further review/tournament-style
> session in the near future?

## Required work

1. Accept RL203 and the complete inherited proof/correction ledgers under the
   verification-economy rule. Do not recursively re-audit 200+ sessions.
2. Build a current closure map from the latest authoritative state: what is
   actually proved, what is only necessary/certified/evidence, and what exact
   obligations still separate the work from RL closure.
3. Assess the current H21/oriented-lift/joint-prefix/root-anchor programme:
   recent rate of genuine constraint, remaining information bottlenecks,
   dependency on unowned completion data, and a realistic chain from the present
   target to a Gate/branch contradiction.
4. Compare it against the strongest still-viable alternative routes already
   identified by prior global audits/route tournaments. Use targeted lookup of
   those summaries only; do not reopen dead routes without a new reason.
5. Rank the next 3-5 research directions by expected closure leverage, proof
   ownership, tractability, duplication risk and cost.
6. Decide one of:
   - continue the present route immediately after RL204;
   - continue it for a bounded number of sessions with explicit success/failure
     criteria, then tournament;
   - pivot now to a broader tournament/route-generation session.
7. If a tournament/review is recommended soon, specify exactly when and what
   evidence would trigger it.
8. Produce a concise roadmap and successor target that preserves every viable
   mathematical thread. No current theorem, certificate or target is discarded
   merely because it is not selected as the immediate next attack.

## Guardrails

This session may re-rank strategy but must not blur proof classifications,
silently promote branch-local work to global closure, or turn "promising" into
"proved". Preserve the exact correction/demotion ledger and all ownership,
physical-incidence, Gate and global scope locks.

# RL204 fresh-unpack review

Date: 2026-08-31. Candidate closeout review.

RL204 is a strategic generation with no new mathematical theorem or finite mathematical
certificate. The portable verifier checks proof-state invariants, exact inherited counts,
strategy classification, H21 preservation, and the unique RL205 tournament decision.

its internal SHA256 manifest checked, and the portable fast verifier run from the clean
unpack. Promotion additionally requires the incoming authority snapshot and BASE_HEAD to
remain unchanged, followed by one atomic tree/commit/ref advance and remote readback.

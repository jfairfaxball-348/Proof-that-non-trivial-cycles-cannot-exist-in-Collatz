# RL159 session state / RL160 kickoff

RL159 proves that the joint distinguished-root ideal is exact but tautological.

For `M=A-L`, `d=|2^A-3^L|`, `B1=2T^L-1`, `B2=3T^M-2`, and the physical `rho`, the `A x A` Sylvester row lattice satisfies

`row_Z(S(B1,B2)) = ker(Q -> Q(rho) mod d)`.

Therefore

`SNF(S)=diag(1,...,1,d)`

and, after appending any `P` of degree `<A`,

`delta_A(S;P)=gcd(d,P(rho))`.

For the inherited dense primitive, retaining the full `d`-component is exactly the original physical condition `P_h(rho)=0 mod d`. The joint elimination branch is therefore frozen as a **method barrier**, not a new exclusion theorem.

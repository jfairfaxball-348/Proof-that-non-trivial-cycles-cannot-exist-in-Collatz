- approximately RL0-RL100: what foundational objects, local obstructions,
  ownership notions, and global arithmetic were established; what failed;
- approximately RL100-RL200: what quotient/content, Farey, local-radius,
  denominator, and global-cycle lessons were learned;

  theorem, and RL313 terminal/full-activity consumers and barriers.

The exact historical boundaries may be adjusted if the repository naturally
clusters differently, but the audit must recover the lessons of early, middle,
and late research rather than treating recent work as the whole project.

### 4. Overlap and shortcut search

The core strategic question is:

> Did later work create a splice that makes an older theorem or route much
> stronger than it looked when originally studied?

Explicitly test overlap between eras.

Examples of legitimate audit questions:

- can RL313 integer row-boundary packing feed an older full-D or quotient
  theorem without falling into RL20?
- do RL310/RL311 extremal bounds now supply an encounter hypothesis that older
  Radius 3/4/5 work lacked?
- does the current controlled-multiplicity branch incidentally make an older
  finite theorem genuinely uniform?
- do P/Q commutation identities combine with later ownership facts in a way
  unavailable when P/Q was frozen?
- can an old Gate-A dependency now be closed incidentally by a theorem proved
  for Gate B/global work?
- are two apparently different hard fronts actually the same missing lemma in
  different coordinates?

Do not assume any answer is yes.  The point is to look systematically.

### 5. Candidate-route ranking

After the audit, rank the best next research routes.

For each serious candidate give:

- exact starting theorem(s);
- exact missing lemma / arrow;
- why the route is parent-near;
- what genuinely independent resource it would exploit;
- what historical barrier it must avoid;
- a fast falsification / stop condition;
- what would count as `EASIER`, not merely another identity.

Prefer a small ranked set over a long brainstorm.

divisor subbranches, and old dependency nodes.

one local calculation derail completion of the audit itself.

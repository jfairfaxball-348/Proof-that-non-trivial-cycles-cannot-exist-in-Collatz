# RL142 completion review

RL142 extends RL141 from non-wrapping to cyclic consecutive height-one contact
interfaces. A block-boundary rotation may shift the defect levels to `{-1,0}`;
the exact shifted numerator identity is included rather than assuming the
nonnegative hypothesis survives.

Scattered deviations, long interfaces, low exterior-block count, other defect
classes, multiplicities, frontiers, and global gates remain open.

## Target worked

## Main result

RL220 obtains the requested useful negative success.

The new fixed-block rigidity theorem proves that if a fixed backward block
containing an odd inverse is repeated indefinitely from a fixed integer
boundary and every iterate remains integral, then the boundary must be the
block's fixed point. Consequently a one-star family is either constant
(non-dyadic star) or a pure dyadic scaling family (D-only star).

The theorem extends to finite rectangular bounded-star expressions: every
star containing an odd inverse is algebraically idle. After those stars are
removed, all genuine parameters occur only in dyadic gaps between a fixed
number of odd inverses. RL218's exact endpoint formula then reduces the full
RL pullback to a finite exponential/S-unit Diophantine equation.

This strictly narrows the surviving route: an RL221 family must have genuinely
growing odd count, correlated/nonrectangular parameters, or aperiodic/recursive
word structure, unless it brings an independent theorem solving the resulting
S-unit image.

## Exact finite route screens

Two tempting shortcuts were checked exactly against the inherited arithmetic
family.

1. Every one of the 45,045 live prefix root progressions has base
   `y_* == 3 (mod 4)`. Because the progression step is `3*2^58`, every root in
   every such progression also satisfies `v2(3y+1)=1`. Therefore the
   externally verified interval `[1,2^71]` cannot be reached in one accelerated
   odd step from any current root. This deletes no candidate and is consistent
   with RL219's stronger conditional physical floor.
2. The explicit certified-blue family `b_n=(4^n-1)/3` has
   `b_38=25185954575304774473045`, which lies in the exact root band
   `[24913843845551577787381,31285589992934194300574]`. An exact scan over all 45,045 live inherited root windows
   finds zero same-integer matches even before the phase-51 selector. Moreover
   this family is `O(2^(2n-1))`, so it is a disguised fixed-word image of a
   dyadic seed ray and was already structurally covered by RL219-T2.

Both are exact finite support results, not new global blue theorems.

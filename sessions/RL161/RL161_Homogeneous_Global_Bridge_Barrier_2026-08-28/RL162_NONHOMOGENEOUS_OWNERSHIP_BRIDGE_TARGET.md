# RL162 — non-homogeneous ownership bridge target

Prove or refute an exact bridge from full ordinary ownership to a global
quantitative restriction that cannot survive generalized-increment scaling.

Priority order:

1. an exact integer-content/lattice condition involving multiple physical
   phases, demonstrably independent of `D | Q`;
2. ordinary Collatz basin/predecessor membership with a global quantitative
   consequence;
3. an absolute physical-capacity or period theorem uniform over every
   remaining branch.

Promotion requires either an exact bridge removing a real global obligation,
or an exact obstruction theorem that eliminates one of these major classes
with its scope precisely stated.  Global non-trivial-cycle exclusion remains
open unless all inherited obligations are discharged.

# RL79 target — post-tournament common-barrier synthesis and new-route generation

Date: 2026-08-24

## Purpose

RL76–RL78 have now seriously exercised the three highest-ranked deliberate architectures coming out of the RL75 route tournament without closing RL:

- the owned macro / pump packing route encountered an unbounded physical-scale/context reservoir;
- product/growth/CF + modern full phase sharpened structure but again left global scale/context unbounded, and the canonical two-pump Diophantine coordinates collapsed to a unimodular change of basis;
- `D|Q`-sensitive bounded-radius reconnaissance produced a clean ownership-defect invariant, but normalized multi-rotation differences are universal transport identities, while the corrected strict-excursion strip cost is again scale-sensitive.

RL79 is therefore a **route reset at theorem architecture level**, not another extension of one exhausted calculation.

## Frozen barriers that every new route must pass

### Barrier 1 — coboundary / transport

Reject any candidate that reduces to:

- `D*(state difference)`;
- the RL48 proper-factor identity;
- the RL65 raw phase quotient;
- the RL20 canonical block coboundary;
- RL78 normalized rotation unit transport.

### Barrier 2 — physical scale

Reject any bound that can be defeated merely by increasing:

- a physical pump-entry/exit state;
- RL76's free integer scale;
- a complementary context coefficient;
- a canonical positive-imbalance height that discounts normalized cost.

A successful theorem must either bound scale, cancel scale exactly while retaining a non-tautological integer obstruction, or work with a genuinely scale-free discrete invariant.

### Barrier 3 — fake compatibility

Use the exact RL20 radius-4 fake as mandatory negative control. It has:

- the relevant local root/final-return grammar;
- positive rational least fixed orbit;
- all-rotation minimum radius `4`;
- `gcd(D,Q)=1`, hence maximal ownership defect `s=D`.

Any proposed global bridge must fail on it for a mathematically visible reason tied to `s=1` / integer ownership, not merely because a numerical parameter happens to differ.

## Primary task

Re-score the still-live RL75 routes after RL76–RL78 and generate at least three theorem-sized architectures that are genuinely independent of the barriers above. For each candidate:

1. state the exact theorem that would advance or close a branch;
2. identify its load-bearing inherited lemmas;
3. derive the first nontrivial formula, not just a concept sketch;
4. apply all three barriers immediately;
5. classify it as live, dead, or needing a sharply named missing lemma.

## Candidate mechanism families worth serious testing

These are invitations, not assumptions.

### A. Absolute ownership / integrality rather than pairwise rotation differences

RL78 shows the common reduced denominator

`s=|D|/gcd(|D|,Q)`

is rotation-invariant and genuine ownership is exactly `s=1`.

Seek a theorem that combines `s=1` with least-state ordering, parity/coprime-6 packing, or phase residue restrictions to force a discrete obstruction. Avoid normalized rotation differences, which are universal.

Examples to test:

- integer-lattice spacing of several simultaneously owned near-minimum phases;
- determinant/minor constructions whose vanishing modulo `D` is **not** implied by unit transport;
- divisibility of a scale-cancelled nonlinear expression that remains nonzero and small.

### B. Prime/order structure of the full denominator

Instead of treating `D|Q` as one congruence, factor conceptually prime-by-prime and ask whether full ownership forces incompatible multiplicative orders of the rotation multipliers, sparse flow polynomials, or phase residues.

A successful route must not merely reconstruct the RL18 orbit polynomial or a resultant already known to telescope.

### C. Global integer permutation/spacing invariants

A genuine cycle gives an entire set of **distinct positive integers** permuted by the shortcut map. The RL20 fake gives only rational phases with common large denominator.

Search for a scale-normalized packing or determinant that uses integrality of many phases simultaneously and cannot be made cheap by scaling every state upward.

### D. Nonlinear cross-rotation invariants

RL78 kills linear unit-normalized differences. Test whether quadratic or higher minors, cross-ratios, or products of independently normalized rotation data cancel the common residue but leave a new factor tied specifically to `Q==0 modD`.

Immediately reject any expression that algebraically factors through the universal transport relation.

### E. Revisit global analytic/Diophantine tools only with a new discrete consumer

Baker/LMN/S-unit/continued-fraction or p-adic tools are permitted only if a newly derived RL-specific discrete restriction makes them finite/uniform. Do not run another generic approximation scan.

## Desired output

The ideal RL79 outcome is one of:

1. a new analytic theorem closing Gate A, Gate B, or a major global branch;
2. a genuinely independent invariant with a precise one-lemma gap to closure;
3. a rigorous route-elimination theorem that shrinks the search space and leaves a re-ranked small set of architectures.

## Stop rule

If every generated candidate collapses to the three common barriers, freeze that fact as a **post-tournament impossibility map**. Do not silently return to q-digits, generic radius enumeration, or CF. The following target should then seek outside the existing RL coordinate system rather than repeatedly reparameterizing the same closure identities.

## Close-out requirement

Freeze exact proofs, failed candidates, recovered interfaces, correction ledger and verifier status; create the next numbered authoritative bundle and sidecar; verify the internal manifest and fresh-unpack fast suite.

# RL206 target — `D|Q`-sensitive owned Gate-B quotient residual

Date: 2026-08-31

RL206 is a focused mathematical theorem attack selected by the RL205 tournament.

## Central target

Build an **exhaustive Gate-B bridge** from full-`D` ownership to the already-useful local
close-pair/strict-excursion geometry by using information that is genuinely unavailable to
the historical `D∤Q` fake.

The preferred information layer is the owned integer quotient

`R_m = Q_m / D`

(or an exactly equivalent physical integer-state observable).

## Phase 1 — recover the live interface, not the archive

Recover only the exact inherited definitions needed for:

- the canonical Gate-B witness / close-pair condition;
- the relevant modulus or packing scale `M`;
- the strict-excursion / radius-3 conclusion to be manufactured;
- the RL20 `D∤Q` fake used as the local countermodel;
- the coboundary/proper-factor barriers.

Freeze those definitions in the RL206 working note before deriving a new residual.

## Phase 2 — quotient-sensitive residual construction

Construct the smallest natural residual family `W` on a canonical owned pair/arc with all
of the following properties:

1. `W` is integer-valued only because full ownership gives `D|Q` and hence integer
   quotient-layer states, or its key divisibility conclusion uses that fact essentially;
2. the corresponding premise fails on the RL20 `D∤Q` fake;
3. `W` is not merely a same-modulus polynomial in rotation numerators `Q_m mod D`;
4. `W` is not a cyclic coboundary or proper-factor endpoint potential;
5. the full-modulus divisibility/packing statement for `W` is proved from the exact
   recovered interface, not guessed from notation.

The inherited quotient transport may be used:

`2^m R_m - 3^P_m R_0 = Q(prefix_m)`,

but restating ordinary orbit recurrence is not progress.

## Phase 3 — contradiction or exhaustive reduction

Primary success target:

- prove the exact modulus conclusion for a canonical witness;
- prove `W != 0`;
- prove the corresponding strict bound, ideally `0 < |W| < M`;
- conclude that the canonical Gate-B witness cannot exist at the proved scope.

Equivalent success is allowed if the geometry instead yields a finite set of bounded
configurations **and** every Gate-B witness is proved to enter that set, so an exact finite
certificate can exhaust it.

- **RL20 fake:** fail at an essential ownership premise.
- **RL79 rank-one:** no pure modulo-`D` rotation-algebra second direction.
- **Coboundary:** no telescoping all-word identity.

  normalization.
- **Scale:** no contradiction that assumes an unproved uniform bound on free context.
- **Globality:** do not call a local or branch-only result Gate B.

## One-session measurable success

Success if RL206 proves one of:

1. a quotient-sensitive, non-coboundary residual with exact full-modulus divisibility and
   a strict contradiction for the exhaustive canonical witness class;
2. a rigorous finite bounded reduction for all Gate-B witnesses;
3. a no-go theorem showing the entire natural quotient-residual family collapses to a
   known barrier, together with a precise reason to pivot.

If (3), set RL207 to H21 independent-information completion rather than trying cosmetic
variants of the same residual family.

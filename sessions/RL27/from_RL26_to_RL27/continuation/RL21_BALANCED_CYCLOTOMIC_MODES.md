# RL21 — balanced-return cyclotomic modes

Date: 2026-08-20

## Status

Sections 1--4 are **ANALYTIC**. Section 5 records **EXACT FINITE EVIDENCE / A COUNTEREXAMPLE TO AN OVERSTRONG VARIANT**.

This note generalizes the `g=2` proper-factor decomposition.  A balanced canonical return does not merely reproduce full-`D` rotation divisibility: after removing the common density factor it exposes a proper cyclotomic cofactor whose quotient is the actual integer state gap.  When the balanced shift has order `3`, the cofactor is exactly the Eisenstein/cubic factor used in the closed radius-3 work.

The main strategic point is therefore positive but limited:

> the cubic factor from radius 3 is a genuine global **relative-state mode** of a three-way balanced decomposition;
>
> however the relative cyclotomic factor does not by itself control the absolute state level.  An additional `X-Y` (sum/average) mode remains.

## 1. General two-segment balanced-return gap cofactor

Write

`A=g a`, `L=g ell`, `X=2^a`, `Y=3^ell`, `D=X^g-Y^g`.

Suppose the least-state word is cut at a canonical balanced cut `j`, so the first segment has length `ja` and weight `j ell`.  Write

`d=uv`,

`U=Q(u)`, `V=Q(v)`,

and let `R` be the state at the root and `x` the state at the cut.  Then

`X^j x - Y^j R = U`,                                      (R21C.1)

`X^(g-j) R - Y^(g-j) x = V`.                              (R21C.2)

Eliminating `R,x` gives

`D (x-R) = (X^(g-j)-Y^(g-j)) U - (X^j-Y^j) V`.            (R21C.3)

Put

`h=gcd(g,j)`,

`M_h=X^h-Y^h`.

Because `h` divides both `j` and `g-j`, `M_h` divides both coefficients on the right and also `D`.  Therefore

`C_gap := (X^g-Y^g)/(X^h-Y^h)`

satisfies the exact identity

`C_gap (x-R)`
` = [(X^(g-j)-Y^(g-j))/M_h] U`
`   - [(X^j-Y^j)/M_h] V`.                                 (R21C.4)

For an actual cycle `x-R` is an integer.  Thus the large proper cofactor `C_gap` divides the weighted block-numerator difference on the right.  This is the correct generalization of the `g=2` factor `X+Y | U-V`.

The shift order of the cut in the `g` canonical blocks is

`n=g/h`.

When `n=3`,

`C_gap = X^(2h)+X^h Y^h+Y^(2h)`.                          (R21C.5)

This is exactly the homogeneous cubic/Eisenstein cofactor.

## 2. Complementary sum mode when the balanced shift order is even

Still put `n=g/h`.  If `n` is even, then `j/h` and `(g-j)/h` are both odd.  Hence

`P_h=X^h+Y^h`

divides both `X^j+Y^j` and `X^(g-j)+Y^(g-j)`, and it divides `D`.

Adding the two state numerators gives

`D (x+R)`
` = (X^(g-j)+Y^(g-j)) U + (X^j+Y^j) V`.                  (R21C.6)

Therefore

`C_sum := D/P_h`

obeys

`C_sum (x+R)`
` = [(X^(g-j)+Y^(g-j))/P_h] U`
`   + [(X^j+Y^j)/P_h] V`.                                 (R21C.7)

Because `X` is even and `Y` odd,

`gcd(X^h-Y^h, X^h+Y^h)=1`.                                (R21C.8)

For order `n=2` this reduces exactly to the RL21 half-balanced decomposition:

`(X+Y)(x-R)=U-V`,

`(X-Y)(x+R)=U+V`.

For higher even order the two cofactors overlap in the remaining cyclotomic factors, so they are not a complete coprime split in the same simple way as `n=2`.

## 3. Three equal balanced macroblocks: exact Eisenstein Fourier decomposition

Now assume a stronger three-way balanced configuration.  Let

`B=2^b`, `C0=3^e`,

and split the word into three consecutive macroblocks

`d=uvw`,

all of length `b` and weight `e`.  Put

`U=Q(u)`, `V=Q(v)`, `W=Q(w)`,

`M=B-C0`,

`C3=B^2+B C0+C0^2`,

`D=B^3-C0^3=M C3`.

Let the three block-start states be `R,x,y`.  Then

`B x-C0 R=U`,

`B y-C0 x=V`,

`B R-C0 y=W`.                                               (R21C.9)

### Absolute mode

Adding gives

`M (R+x+y)=U+V+W`.                                         (R21C.10)

Modulo `M`, the full numerator satisfies

`Q(d) == C0^2 (U+V+W) (mod M)`,                            (R21C.11)

so `M|Q(d)` is exactly the absolute/sum mode.

### Relative cubic mode

Let `omega^2+omega+1=0` in the Eisenstein integers.  Define

`F_state = R + omega x + omega^2 y`,

`F_Q = U + omega V + omega^2 W`.

A direct discrete-Fourier calculation from (R21C.9) gives

`F_Q = (B omega^2-C0) F_state`.                             (R21C.12)

The Eisenstein norm of the multiplier is

`N(B omega^2-C0)=B^2+B C0+C0^2=C3`.                       (R21C.13)

Equivalently, modulo `C3`, the image `omega -> B/C0` identifies the relative condition

`C3 | Q(d)`

with Eisenstein divisibility of `F_Q` by the prime/principal factor generated by `B omega^2-C0` (up to a unit/conjugation convention).

Since `gcd(M,C3)=1` here (`M` is nonzero modulo `3`), full `D` divisibility splits into

1. the absolute factor `M | U+V+W`, and
2. the relative cubic/Eisenstein factor modulo `C3`.

For an actual integer cycle, (R21C.12) further gives the exact norm identity

`N(F_Q)=C3 * N(F_state)`.                                  (R21C.14)

Because `1+omega+omega^2=0`,

`F_state = omega(x-R)+omega^2(y-R)`,

so

`N(F_state)`
` = (x-R)^2-(x-R)(y-R)+(y-R)^2`.                           (R21C.15)

Thus the quotient of the cubic norm is the positive-definite Eisenstein norm of the two physical low-state gaps.

This is the cleanest structural connection found so far between the global balanced-return programme and the radius-3 cubic/Eisenstein machinery.

## 4. What this does and does not give

If the canonical imbalance path has equally spaced zeros at one-third and two-thirds of the cycle, then the three equal-density macroblocks above are available.  The cubic factor `C3` is not an artificial local modulus: it is exactly the relative Fourier mode of those three balanced low states.

A successful global bridge could therefore try to prove a **relative cubic uniqueness theorem** under the actual least-state/ownership geometry, forcing

`U=V=W`.

Since `Q` is injective for fixed block length and weight (recover the word bit-by-bit from `Q`), this would force

`u=v=w`,

making the full word a nonprimitive third repeat.

However, no such infinite relative uniqueness theorem is proved here.  Moreover the `C3` mode alone does not control the absolute state level; the coprime `M=B-C0` sum mode remains indispensable, exactly as the `X+Y` gap factor alone was insufficient in the `g=2` case.

## 5. Finite red-team results

The verifier `verify_rl21_balanced_cyclotomic_cofactor.py` checks the general gap identity, the even-order sum identity, and the order-3 cubic specialization exhaustively on a small exact domain.

A separate search initially suggested that the cubic relative congruence might force `U=V=W` for all equal-density blocks.  That statement is false without ownership geometry.  An exact non-diagonal counterexample occurs at

`b=10`, `e=6`, `B=1024`, `C0=729`,

with blocks having one-position sets

`u: (0,1,3,4,6,7)`,

`v: (0,2,3,5,6,8)`,

`w: (3,4,5,6,7,8)`.

Their numerators are

`U=1085`, `V=1519`, `W=5320`,

and the full three-block numerator is divisible by `C3` but not by `M`.  The associated rational fixed orbit is not least at the displayed block root, and two of the three blocks fail the supercritical-prefix ownership geometry.

So the raw cubic-relative statement is retired.  The viable theorem target is explicitly:

> **cubic relative uniqueness under genuine least-state/balanced-return ownership**, not for arbitrary equal-density blocks.

This is a narrower and structurally justified place to try to reuse the radius-3 Eisenstein methods.

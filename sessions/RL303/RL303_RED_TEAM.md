Rejected. The exact dyadic lift is valid, but the required linear-budget checkpoint-8 entry inequality is not a universal theorem. This shortcut is not promoted.

Rejected. Four large exact merger families were found, but no complete prefix cut was proved. O1 remains open.

Rejected by inherited RL302 counterexamples. RL303 instead localizes the first P-wall hit and then proves a stronger barrier against a generic linear-credit debt theorem.

Decisively rejected. For `D=6n+3`, the legal physical pair

`W_(D,4)1^(3n)` versus `W_(D,0)(110)^n`

has source-minus-owner cost `-3n(n-1)/2`, quadratic in `n`. Thus debt size plus linear-in-D reserve is not a sufficient invariant.

Corrected. On the root source prefix `R3 011`, checkpoint 8 already owns the identical `RW_3` wall one unit more cheaply. The renewal machinery is therefore not the right root theorem.

Not promoted. The algebraic factorisation is easy, but no uniform cheap checkpoint-8 access to the required higher lattice cells was proved.

### 7. P/Q commutation already closes the leading-P branch

Not claimed. RL303 proves one exact physical swap and an all-r Q-return family; a complete repeated-stack grammar remains the successor target.

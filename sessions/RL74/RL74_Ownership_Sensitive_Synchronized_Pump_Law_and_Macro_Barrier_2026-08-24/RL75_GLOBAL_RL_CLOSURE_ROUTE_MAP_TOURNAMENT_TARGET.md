## Required route families

### 1. Gate A — rank / height / area closure

Identify the strongest proved sector, first open range, exact missing theorem, and whether the obstruction is local/global/ownership/zero-depth. Reassess whether RL63–RL74 changes the outlook for a uniform `H>=k`-type theorem.

### 2. Gate B — radius / owned-rotation bridge

Reassess genuine RL object -> sufficiently close owned rotations -> closed finite-radius sector -> contradiction. Do **not** assume radius must be 3. Ask whether genuine ownership/full phase/divisibility/packing can force an absolute `radius<=R0`, and separately what local radius 4,5,6,... closure would then be needed.

### 3. Direct full-phase arithmetic contradiction

Seek a genuinely new normalized residual/divisibility/sign/size incompatibility, structurally like `D|W` with `0<|W|<D`, but reject rearrangements of the raw `(2-N)M` identity or known coboundaries.

### 4. Global invariant / defect-energy route

Search for sign-definite, monotone, conserved-vs-forced, periodicity-incompatible, or two-sided incompatible global quantities using defect energy, rank displacement, weighted population, ownership, terminal area, prefix weights, phase selectors, and state packing.

### 5. Minimal-counterexample / descent route

Require an explicit transformation, a well-founded decreasing quantity, and preservation of legality, full divisibility, full phase and primitiveness where required. RL74's determinant-zero pump deletion hook belongs here and must be audited against the genuine primitive-RL definition.

### 6. Product / growth / weighted-population route

Recombine RL19/RL20 weighted populations, positive-lift identities, product bounds, state packing, numerator bounds and rotation-weighted differences with modern RL64+ ownership/full phase and RL65–RL74 terminal/macro information.

### 7. Global combinatorial packing / cyclic-word route

Treat the full cyclic word globally. Examine packing between owned rotations, forced close pairs, density/support spacing, cyclic discrepancy, forbidden large-separation configurations, and whether giant zero-area macros themselves force global packing excess.

### 8. Diophantine / approximation route

Use the exceptional `2^a ~ 3^ell` approximation **plus RL-specific structure** (phase, defects, divisibility, ownership, cyclic words, quotient classes). RL74's competing shrink/growth pump ratios `(8/9)^p(4/3)^q` are a concrete new two-scale Diophantine interface worth testing.

### 9. Genuine 2-adic / 3-adic incompatibility

Further digits count only if a global theorem consumes them. Seek an actual incompatibility among 2-adic ownership cylinders, 3-adic phase selectors, positivity/size and the finite integer/global object—not more compatible digits.

### 10. Hybrid closure routes

Deliberately test pairs that may become decisive together: defect+ownership, radius+rank area, full phase+weighted population, rank displacement+packing, p-adic selectors+size, terminal exact values+denominator, local radius+full `D|Q`, zero-depth control+defect lower bounds, and RL74 pump law+descent/packing.

RL75 may add serious route families not listed here.

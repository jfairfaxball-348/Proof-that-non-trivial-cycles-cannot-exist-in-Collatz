# RL119 target — conditioned p-adic transfer

Define a finite-level operator on actual ordinary owned rotation fibres,
conditioned on complete `D|Q` ownership rather than random parity. Specify
the finite p-adic state, transition probabilities or deterministic fibres,
and a non-random stationary law. Prove an escape or contraction statement that
forces selected support away from the RL110 dangerous coefficient cone.

The first lemma must show why the conditioned owned-fibre operator is finite
and differs from the unconditioned parity model without replacing complete
ownership by a selected factor of `D`. It must reject RL20 at `D∤Q`, retain
ordinary unscaled divisibility against RL79, avoid quotient-as-state at RL81,
locate primitivity, and state all scopes and finite bounds.

Stop if conditioning needs the full unbounded denominator or factorization,
if stationarity is heuristic, or if the operator reduces to random/unowned
parity. Then begin the non-homogeneous cocycle diagnostic.

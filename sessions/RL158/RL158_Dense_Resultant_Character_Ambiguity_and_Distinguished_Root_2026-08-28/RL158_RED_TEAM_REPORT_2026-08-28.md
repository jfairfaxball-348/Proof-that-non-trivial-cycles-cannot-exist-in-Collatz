# RL158 red-team report

- The counterexample uses the corrected RL157 polynomial and a valid nonnegative excursion; it does not revive the demoted `3T^L-2` normalization.
- `D | Res(2T^L-1,P_h)` is retained only as a necessary condition, never as a converse.
- The exact counterexample has `Res=D`, so failure cannot be repaired by demanding only one copy of each prime factor.
- The two prime factors are supplied by different nonphysical roots; the physical evaluation is nonzero modulo both factors.
- The companion `3T^(A-L)-2` is derived from the same physical phase and is not the demoted `3T^L-2` polynomial.
- The joint two-binomial interface is not claimed to be a new exclusion theorem. RL159 must test whether it gives arithmetic beyond a tautological restatement of ownership.

# RL103 target — non-homogeneous unit-increment lattice content at the first-Farey cylinder

## Purpose

RL103 continues the unresolved first-Farey raw-multiplicity problem after
RL102 froze direct full-word transport and homogeneous strict-excursion
packing.  Gate A and Gate B remain open; no global closure is claimed.

The retained live input is that a genuine raw-`g` first-surplus maximum is
the least positive even representative of a cylinder modulo `2*3^(gq)` and
has exponentially tiny normalized height.  RL103 must seek an additional
constraint that uses the absolute ordinary odd increment `+1`, equivalently
the ownership-defect condition `s=1`, in a way unavailable to generalized
`T_s` cycles.

## Primary target

Construct or rule out a non-homogeneous lattice/content coupling among:

- the maximum cylinder residue modulo `2*3^(gq)`;
- the exact integer phase equations at the first-surplus cut and at one or
  more genuinely owned full-cycle rotations;
- full `D|Q(W_full)`; and
- the absolute unit increment, before any normalization by a common scale.

Acceptable success is an all-raw-`g>=2` lower-content theorem, a bounded
nonzero multiple of a denominator, a finite residual set with exact branch
parameters, or a new `s=1`-specific finite/elimination mechanism.

## Required exclusions and tests

1. A construction that is invariant under `(states,s)->(c states,c s)` is
   not a success; identify the non-homogeneous `s=1` step explicitly.
2. Do not reuse `2^mQ(rot_mW)-3^{P_m}Q(W)=D Q(prefix)` as an independent
   content condition; RL102 proves its first-surplus specialization is only
   transport.
3. Do not use a normalized coordinate as a physical interval bound.
4. Test every candidate against the RL20 `D not-dividing Q` fake and the
   RL102 generalized excursion witness.
5. Preserve raw `(gp,gq)` versus reduced `(p,q)` scope and the external-floor
   label when used.

## Failure / pivot

Freeze RL103 if every proposed lattice condition reduces to a scaled
generalized-increment identity, to a rotation transport/coboundary, or to an
unbounded denominator floor.  If frozen, rerank a direct Gate-B
denominator-content architecture before any bulk cascade resumption.

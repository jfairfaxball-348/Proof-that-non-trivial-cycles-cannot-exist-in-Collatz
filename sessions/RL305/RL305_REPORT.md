## 4. Exact scalar decomposition of the P bottleneck

Use RL302 notation:

- `P=(2,J=3,K=6)`;
- checkpoint `8=(1,J=8,K=9)`;
- tower source `R3=(3,K=27)` (called `R3_tower` below when ambiguity matters);
- `D0=(3,J=17,K=24)`;
- `A=(2,J=4,K=7)`.

For every positive even checkpoint `E!=2`, RL302 proves

`m_2(E)=1+min(m_R3(E),m_8(E))`,

`m_A(E)=1+min(m_2(E),m_D0(E))`,

`m_P(E)=1+min(m_A(E),m_2(E))`.

Pure min-plus algebra therefore gives

`boxed: m_P(E)=2+min(m_R3(E),m_8(E),m_D0(E))`.

Let

`Bcal(X)=sup_E [nu_2(E)-m_X(E)]`

over positive-even checkpoint endpoints.

For `E!=2`,

`nu_2(E)-m_P(E)
 = max(
   nu_2(E)-m_R3(E),
   nu_2(E)-m_8(E),
   nu_2(E)-m_D0(E)
 ) - 2`.

The exceptional endpoint `E=2` cannot affect the final supremum:

- its valuation is `1` and all source costs are nonnegative, so its contribution to any source envelope is at most `1`;
- checkpoint 8 itself gives `Bcal(8)>=3`;
- P reaches checkpoint 2 with nonnegative cost, while the non-2 part already gives P score at least `1` via the exact P-to-8 cost-2 path.

Hence exactly

`boxed: Bcal(P)=max(Bcal(R3),Bcal(8),Bcal(D0))-2`.

Therefore

`boxed:
Bcal(P)<=1
iff
Bcal(R3)<=3,
Bcal(8)<=3,
Bcal(D0)<=3.`

This is an exact theorem-sized audit reduction.

## 10. Max-plus reinterpretation of RL302–RL304

Define normalized wall excesses

`X_L(D)=Bcal(L_D)-B_L(D)`,
`X_R(D)=Bcal(RW_D)-B_R(D)`.

RL303's exact same-state merger families become weighted Bellman rewrites.

For a same-state pair with source continuation cost `c_s`, owner continuation cost `c_o`, and `delta=c_s-c_o`,

`Bcal(source branch)<=Bcal(owner)-delta`.

Subtracting the source/owner wall credits gives a max-plus transition weight.

The four RL303 D-to-D-2 merger families therefore have normalized weights:

1. `L_D -> RW_(D-2)`: `k-D-4`;
2. `L_D -> L_(D-2)`: `k-2D-5`;
3. `RW_D -> L_(D-2)`: `k-3D-2`;
4. `RW_D -> RW_(D-2)`: `k-2D-8`;

on their exact stated legal prefix families.

The adjacent-wall normal form

`L_D 0 1^k 01 = RW_D 1^(k+1)00`

has normalized weight

`D-k-1`

from the L family to the adjacent R family.

These formulas are exact consequences of the already-promoted RL302/RL303 cost differences and wall credits. They do not constitute a complete prefix cover or a closure theorem.

Their significance is strategic: a grammar need not terminate syntactically if every directed cycle loses normalized Bellman excess. This is the first falsifiable convergence measure identified for the recent wall/PQ algebra.

## 12. Falsifiable stopping/pivot criterion

The Bellman-wall programme may continue only if it produces objective complexity decrease.

A result counts as convergence if it does at least one of:

1. closes one of the seven exact scalar Gate-A obligations;
2. reduces an infinite scalar family to a finite complete transducer;
3. proves a well-founded decreasing potential;
4. proves every cycle in the normalized weighted transducer has negative total Bellman-excess weight;
5. converts every residual branch into an already bounded scalar sink;
6. strictly reduces the number/type of unresolved scalar families under a gap-free cover.

A result does **not** count as convergence merely because it gives:

- another coordinate change;
- another exact local commutation identity;
- another finite grammar with no weighted termination measure;
- another larger bounded scan;
- another same-endpoint owner identity on an uncovered subset.

Concrete pivot trigger for RL306:

If the exact normalized wall/PQ/D0 grammar contains an iteratable nonnegative-weight cycle after correct credits, or cannot be closed to finitely many family states without reintroducing unrestricted checkpoint futures, freeze it and pivot back to the direct ancestry/static-danger formulation. Do not respond by adding another unweighted commutation layer.

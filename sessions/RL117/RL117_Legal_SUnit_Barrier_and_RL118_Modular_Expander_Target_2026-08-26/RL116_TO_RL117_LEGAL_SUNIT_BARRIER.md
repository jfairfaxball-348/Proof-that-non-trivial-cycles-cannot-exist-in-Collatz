# RL117 — legal-coordinate S-unit barrier and route transition

## Outcome and classification

RL117 does not prove an S-unit gap or a terminal reduction. It records a
**method barrier** for the requested legal-coordinate formulation. The
zero-flow cut, the minimum linear swap path, and every local coordinate rule
for a support term are consequences of binary transport alone. In particular,
for a swap at position `i` with `t` ones to its right,

`i+t+2<=A`, `t<=L-1`, and the term is `2^i 3^t`.

These are exactly the all-legal constraints already used by RL110. Path order
can add only transport/order constraints determined by the two binary words;
it contains no `D` or ordinary increment. Full ownership enters only through
`D|Q(u)` and `D|Q(v)`. By the RL114 rotation-ownership identity the second
condition follows from the first; subtracting them gives the already-known
`D|S`. Thus no new coordinate restriction was derived from ownership, and no
two- or three-monomial estimate beyond an all-legal S-unit statement is
available.

RL110 already proves that all-legal support geometry cannot compress `|S|`
below `D` in the `R>=4` complement. A generic S-unit or slope estimate would
therefore not satisfy the RL117 obligation. The route reaches its stated stop
condition and passes to modular-expander lifting.

No Gate closure, cycle exclusion, or Collatz conclusion is claimed.
